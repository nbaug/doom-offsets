What's a microdoom?

A one-in-a-million chance of existential catastrophe. 100 µdoom is one basis point (0.01%).

Caveats & sources

Prices are the full expected harm, like carbon's social cost. Real carbon offsets are priced at the cost of removal, which for x-risk funders put at roughly $2.5M to $20B per basis point.

Thorstad argues high per-century risk caps the value of reducing risk now at about one extra century of humanity. Some safety work may also speed up capabilities.
